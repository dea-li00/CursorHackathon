Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI extraction?

Our hybrid AI engine achieves 95%+ accuracy on standard invoice formats. The system uses multiple extraction methods and always allows manual review and correction.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes! InvoiceIQ is designed with privacy-first principles. All processing happens locally in your browser, and no data is sent to external servers unless you explicitly choose to.

What does it cost?

InvoiceIQ is completely free and open source. No hidden costs, no subscription fees, and no vendor lock-in. You own your data and your workflow.

What Excel format does it export?

We export to standard Excel (.xlsx) format with proper AP bill structure including vendor details, line items, totals, and categorization columns. Next, explicit formats supported by different countries and accounting formats will be supported.

What file types are supported?

We support PDF invoices and common image formats (JPG, PNG, TIFF). The system works best with clear, high-resolution documents.

Does it work in multiple languages?

So far, only English is supported, stay tuned for more languages in the future.

Can I integrate it with my accounting system?

The exported Excel files are designed to be easily imported into most accounting systems. We're also working on direct integrations with popular accounting platforms.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

Every extraction includes a review step where you can correct any errors before export. The system learns from your corrections to improve future accuracy.

Privacy & Data Security

InvoiceIQ is built with privacy-first principles. Here's how we protect your data:

  • Local Processing: All AI processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers.
  • No Analytics: We don't track your usage or collect analytics by default. Your workflow remains completely private.
  • Optional Contact: If you choose to contact us, we use Formspree for form handling with industry-standard security.
  • Open Source: All code is open source, so you can verify exactly how your data is handled.
